Do I need to provide photos?

Yes. Your own job photos and short video clips help us create content that reflects your actual business. You can also send offers, availability and genuine customer reviews through WhatsApp.

Will you actually publish the posts?

Yes. We create the content, write the captions, schedule and publish it to the pages included in your package. We also handle the local Facebook group placements included in your plan.

How does local group posting work?

We identify relevant groups for your service area and submit promotional posts where permitted. One placement means one post submitted to one group. Groups can require admin approval or restrict promotional posting.

Are Facebook and Instagram posts counted separately?

No. A branded post is one unique piece of content. In Local Growth and Complete, that content is shared across both Facebook and Instagram. Reels are included separately as listed.

Do I have to share my Facebook password?

No. We’ll help you grant the appropriate business page access so we can manage the agreed tasks.

Are enquiries and comments included?

The standard packages cover content and publishing. Inbox and comment handling is available separately, with an agreed allowance and response schedule.

Can you run paid Facebook ads too?

Paid advertising is separate from these organic social packages. Ask Kieran about GetMyAds and we can discuss campaign management and the advertising budget.

Is there a long-term contract?

Packages run monthly. We’ll confirm the billing, cancellation arrangements and agreed deliverables with you before you get started.
